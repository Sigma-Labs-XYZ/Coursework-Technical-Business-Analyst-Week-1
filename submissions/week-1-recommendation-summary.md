## Phase 1 Recommendations:

Considering the ranked view analysis and stakeholder needs at Legacy Trust, I would recommend that Phase 1 should implement an escalation automation system that would create higher visibility to both team leaders and agents, decreasing the possibility of high delinquency accounts from being neglected. It has the lowest time to value benefit, implemented in 3 months and a payback period of less than 3 months.

Secondly, an automated case triage and queue escalation would save agents manual hours from spreadsheet reconciliation and avoid accidentally missing a high risk delinquency account. An investment of £10,000 with a payback period of 2.5 months, the investment would very quickly be regained back. 

Thirdly, I recommend implementing an automated follow up update, which will remove the possibility of human error of missed outcome entries, saving agents time on spreadsheet reconciliation and from repeated redundant, repeated contact with customers.

There are other two recommendations that I would like to note to be considered on the phase 2 project. 

An automated cross-channel sync and duplication elimination, this would eliminate duplicate entries and unify the the database system, removing the complex data search that agents currently need to regularly address. Although a pressing matter, the £20,000 investment with a time to value of 13.9 months, it would be best to implement it as a long term goal.

Another very valuable automation I would recommend is a self-service debt payment system for low-risk delinquency accounts, allowing customers to pay for their debts with a set up payment plan, freeing up the agents to work on more complex cases. However, there is a high upfront investment of £45,000 and a payback period of over 9 months and a time to value of 21 months.

These recommendations were raised by Amina Rahman (operational visibility) and Gareth Evans (senior collections team leader).
