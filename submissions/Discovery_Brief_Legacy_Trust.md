## Discovery Brief

## 1. Problem Summary

As Legacy Trust Bank expanded, the lack of a cohesive and unified debt recovery system has become highly critical with 56 agents managing 3,246 delinquent accounts. The informal workarounds that staff implement have become ineffective due to the escalating increase in delinquent accounts. Customers have been neglected due to repetitive contact journeys from agents and inconsistent status updates on each case; this can potentially lead to customer frustration, which can lead Legacy Trust to increase its churn rate and a loss of revenue.

Through analysis, it was revealed that 20.42% of recovery activities are duplicates, totalling 230 hours of work encompassing manual spreadsheet reconciliation and status checks. This directly affects recoveries, operational capacity, and leadership confidence.

## 5. Final Problem Statement

Firstly, I would recommend implementing an automated cross-channel syncing system to remove duplication cases. This will save agents 230 hours from reconciling information and reduce touch point repetition with customers. Secondly, an automated case triage system and queue management, accounts with higher delinquency levels will gain higher visibility by agents.

An escalation automation process that would send email notifications to team leaders about when a delinquent account enters the late delinquency stage and becomes high risk. It will increase visibility from the team leadership.

An automated follow up update would be inserted into the system once contact is achieved with a client, generated to the unified data sheet. This will avoid duplication and confusion for the agents, saving time.

Lastly, a self-service debt payment portal will be implemented for lower risk account holders, giving them the ability to create payment plans and save agents time in order to handle more complex cases.
