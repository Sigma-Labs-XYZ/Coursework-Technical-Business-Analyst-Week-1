## How the current process feels

For a customer:
You contact the bank and start from the beginning every time. Nobody who calls you seems to know what the last person agreed. You are asked the same questions on each call, and you cannot check anything yourself, so you have no idea whether what you were promised was recorded.

For an agent:
Most of the job is working out what has already happened by reading the database, then the spreadsheet, then the email trail, before you can safely pick up the phone and talk to a customer. Even then you might be calling someone that a colleague had spoken to yesterday.

For a team leader:
Nothing tells you which cases are moving and which have stalled, because the same status means different things to different people. Any picture of the team comes from reconciling three sources by hand.

To summarise, the customer cannot see the record, the agent has to rebuild it, and the manager cannot trust.
