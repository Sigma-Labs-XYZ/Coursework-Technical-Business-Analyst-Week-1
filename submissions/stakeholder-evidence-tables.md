# Stakeholder Evidence by Theme

This document groups stakeholder interview evidence into focused themes and adds short impact and confidence notes.

## Confidence scale used

- High: repeated by several stakeholders, specific, and directly observed.
- Medium: clear point, but weaker on one of consistency, specificity, or directness.
- Low: single-source and/or more inferred than directly observed; needs validation.

## Removed Theme: change resistance or distrust

Removed following discussion with Priya: One thought: "Change resistance or distrust" feels like it sits slightly differently from the others. The first five are operational pain points that we can potentially address through process or system changes, but change resistance is more of a context that affects how solutions will land. It's still important to capture, but you might want to think about whether it belongs in the same list or whether it's better treated as a constraint or risk factor when you're shaping recommendations later.
