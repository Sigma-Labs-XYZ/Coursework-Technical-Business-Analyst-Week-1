# Discovery Brief Template

## 1. Problem summary

**What problem is Legacy Trust trying to solve?**
Legacy Trust’s debt recovery operation has not modernised alongside the rest of the bank. More than 50 agents manage over 100,000 delinquent accounts across personal loans, credit cards and auto finance, using spreadsheets, email trails and a 20-year old collections database.This results in missed follow-ups, duplicated activity and inconsistent status updates which directly affects recoveries, operational capacity and leadership confidence. Leadership wants to know if a self-serve portal, which lets customers confirm balances and resolve simple cases without an agent, is a viable Phase 1 investment to fix this.

**Why is the current way of working failing at scale?**
The process was never built as a single, joined-up system. This means that the process depends heavily on individual memory, manual reconciliation and duplicated effort. As a result, as delinquent account volumes increased, those workarounds stopped scaling. Managers lost visibility of which follow-ups had happened, agents developed inconsistent ways of updating statuses and customers were often pushed into slow, repetitive contact journeys.

**What evidence would leadership expect before approving Phase 1?**
Leadership's expectations split across four stakeholders. Amina needs a clear, evidence-based map of where the current process breaks down, plus proof of which automation ideas will save the most time. Daniel wants measurable savings and collections-performance improvements, with any assumptions clearly stated and open to challenge. Gareth wants the As-Is process documented honestly that handles difficult cases and messy hand-offs so that it doesn’t land back on agents. Priya wants the analysis traceable, so every finding links through to an opportunity, a future-state workflow, and a buildable requirement.

**Why does it matter financially?**
Delayed actions and inconsistent follow-up are estimated to contribute to a 15% revenue loss in recoveries. Leadership wants a Phase 1 portal business case that pays back within 12 months, built on transparent, defensible assumptions.

## 3. Discovery questions

1. Which debt recovery steps are high-volume and rules-driven enough to move to self-service, based on current case-type volumes?
2. Where specifically do spreadsheets, email trails, and manual hand-offs create duplicate contact or lost follow-ups and how often does each occur?
3. Which baseline metrics best evidence operational waste and connect to the estimated 15% revenue leakage?
4. How much agent time is spent on reconciliation and status-checking versus judgement-based case work, and what would that time be worth redirected?
5. At which specific points in the current process do delays or missed follow-ups occur most often, and what triggers them?
6. Which automation opportunities show the strongest 12-month ROI and for each one, are we relying on measured baseline data or an assumption, and is that clearly labelled either way?

## 5. Final problem statement

Legacy Trust manages over 100,000 delinquent accounts through unscalable manual workarounds, resulting in duplicated effort and an estimated 15% revenue leakage. We need to determine if investing in a Phase 1 self-service portal will successfully reduce agent handling of straightforward cases, prevent revenue loss, and pay back within 12 months.
