# Stakeholder Evidence by Theme

This document groups stakeholder interview evidence into six themes and adds short impact and confidence notes.

## Confidence scale used

- High: repeated by several stakeholders and/or supported by numbers.
- Medium: clear point, but less repeated or less measurable.

## Theme: poor account-status visibility

| stakeholder | quote or observation | theme | business impact | confidence level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compliance Liaison (SN-015) | A case marked resolved in one system might be in progress in another. | poor account-status visibility | Teams see different statuses for the same case, causing mistakes and rework. | High |
| Jacqueline Norris (Operations Analyst, SN-085) | There is no standard definition of what each status actually means across the team. | poor account-status visibility | Staff interpret statuses differently, so reports and decisions are less reliable. | High |
| Sarah Mitchell (Collections Agent, SN-107) | There is no standard definition of what each status actually means across the team. | poor account-status visibility | Handoffs are less clear, which slows work and creates duplicate checks. | High |
| Dr Louis Sinclair (Service Design Lead, SN-122) | Different agents use different status codes, which makes reporting meaningless. | poor account-status visibility | Management cannot trust status reports to target the right fixes. | High |
| Mr Dale Jackson (Collections Agent, SN-073) | The activity log does not distinguish between actual contact and failed contact attempts. | poor account-status visibility | The team cannot see which contact attempts actually worked. | Medium |
