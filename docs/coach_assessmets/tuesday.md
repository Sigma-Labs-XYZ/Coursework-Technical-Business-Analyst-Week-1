
Situation: 
- 2 decades building a good reputation
- Over 4,000 clients
- Lean and experienced compliance function
- Team knew rules and clients

Complication:
- 47/200 client records were incomplete or had out of date KYC documentation
    - 11 didn't account for a refresh following client material change
    - 3 predated the company's AML policy
- 90 days to demonstrate identification of the full scope of the issue and a credible remediation plan 
- Roughly 1,100 client records had incomplete document metadata.

Q: How can we ensure that the data for each client is consistently compliant with the current regulations?

A: We need a centralised platform that tracks and manages customer data and document metadata which is simple and easy to work with.
