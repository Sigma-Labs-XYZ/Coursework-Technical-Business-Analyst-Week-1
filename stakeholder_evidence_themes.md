# Stakeholder Evidence Themes

The 126 notes below are assigned once to a primary theme. Note IDs preserve traceability; repeated or similar observations from different people remain separate evidence. Every row includes the stakeholder and role, source note ID, verbatim quote or observation, theme, qualitative business impact, and confidence level.

Business impacts are qualitative implications inferred from the interview notes, not measured outcomes. Confidence describes how strongly a theme recurs across this pack; it does not independently verify the underlying claims. Any quoted numerical estimates should be validated against operational or financial data.
